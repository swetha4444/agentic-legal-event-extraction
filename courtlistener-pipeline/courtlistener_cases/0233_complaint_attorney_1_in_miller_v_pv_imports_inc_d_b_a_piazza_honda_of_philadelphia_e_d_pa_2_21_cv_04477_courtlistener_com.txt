                                            COMPLAINT

I.     INTRODUCTION

       Plaintiff, David Miller (“Plaintiff”), brings this action against Defendants, PV Imports,

Inc. d/b/a Piazza Honda of Philadelphia, and Piazza Auto Group (collectively, “Defendants”), for

unlawful age and disability discrimination and retaliation in violation of the Age Discrimination

in Employment Act, as amended, 29 U.S.C. § 621, et seq. (“ADEA”), the Americans with

Disabilities Act, as amended, 42 U.S.C. § 12101, et seq. (“ADA”), the Pennsylvania Human

Relations Act, as amended, 43 P.S. § 951, et seq. (“PHRA”), and the Philadelphia Fair Practices

Ordinance, Phila. Code § 9-1101, et seq. (“PFPO”). Plaintiff also asserts a claim of retaliation

against Defendant in violation of the Family and Medical Leave Act, 29 U.S.C. § 2601, et seq.

(“FMLA”). Plaintiff seeks damages, including economic loss, compensatory, liquidated
         Case 2:21-cv-04477-NIQA Document 1 Filed 10/12/21 Page 5 of 25




damages, punitive damages, attorneys’ fees and costs, and all other relief that this Court deems

appropriate.
