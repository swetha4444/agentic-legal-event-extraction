        Plaintiff, by and through his undersigned counsel, hereby avers as follows:

                                       I.      INTRODUCTION

        1.      This action has been initiated by Carlton Payne (hereinafter referred to as

“Plaintiff”) against Penn Color, Inc. (hereinafter referred to as “Defendant” or “Penn”) for

violations of Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. 2000e) and the Pennsylvania

Human Relations Act (“PHRA”)1. As a direct consequence of Defendant’s unlawful actions,

Plaintiff seeks damages as set forth herein.

claims because this civil action arises under laws of the United States.

knowledge and experience for it.

        16.    Plaintiff performed his job well and was initially issued positive feedback

interacted with another employee named Nassir (last name unknown).

asked Plaintiff, “do you want to see my balls.”

March 2022.

        21.    In response, Plaintiff said, “excuse me” and then tried to ignore Nassir.

        22.    Nassir also repeatedly winked at Plaintiff, told him, “You’re looking good today”

and made other inappropriate sexual comments.

Defendant’s illegal actions, including but not limited to back pay, front pay, salary, pay

increases, bonuses, insurance, and benefits.

suffering, and humiliation caused by Defendant’s actions;

amount believed by the Court or trier of fact to be appropriate to punish Defendant for its willful,
