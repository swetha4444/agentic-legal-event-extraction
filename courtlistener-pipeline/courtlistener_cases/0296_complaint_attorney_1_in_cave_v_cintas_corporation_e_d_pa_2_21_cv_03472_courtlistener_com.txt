                                     COMPLAINT

I.     INTRODUCTION

       Plaintiff, Tara Cave, brings this action against her former employer, Cintas

Corporation (“Defendant”). After Plaintiff demonstrated excellent performance over

fourteen (14) years of employment, Defendant terminated her based on her complaints

about the discriminatory conduct to which it was subjecting her and other black female

employees, and shortly after Plaintiff advised Defendant of her medical conditions and

requested accommodations for the same.

       Defendant’s discriminatory and retaliatory conduct towards Plaintiff violated Title

VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, as amended, 42 U.S.C. § 2000e, et seq. (“Title VII”);

42 U.S.C. §1981 (“Section 1981”); the Americans with Disabilities Act, as amended, 42

U.S.C. § 12101, et seq. (“ADA”); the Pennsylvania Human Relations Act, as amended,

43 P.S. § 951, et seq. (“PHRA”); and, the Philadelphia Fair Practices Ordinance, as

amended, Phila. Code § 9-1100, et seq. (“PFPO”).
