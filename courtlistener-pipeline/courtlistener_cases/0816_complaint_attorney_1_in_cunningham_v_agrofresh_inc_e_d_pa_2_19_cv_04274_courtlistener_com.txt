‘Tt is well established that corporate officers are liable for torts in Pennsylvania in which they participate. In Rowles
v. Automated Prod. Sys., 1999 U.S. Dist. LEXIS 21605 (M.D. Pa. 1999), the court explained any corporate officer is
individually liable for their tortious actions, stating:

In Wicks v. Milzoco Builders, Inc., 503 Pa. 614, 470 A.2d 86, 90 (Pa. 1983), the Pennsylvania
Supreme Court recognized the participation theory as a basis for corporate officer liability:

Under the participation theory, the court imposes liability on the individual as an actor rather than
as an owner. Such liability is not predicated on a finding that the corporation is a sham and a mere
alter ego of the individual corporate officer. Instead, liability attaches where the record establishes
the individual's participation in the tortious activity.

(1) Concerns with improper expenses by CEO

22. Plaintiff vocalized objections to expenditures by Defendant Ferre. Defendant Ferre
