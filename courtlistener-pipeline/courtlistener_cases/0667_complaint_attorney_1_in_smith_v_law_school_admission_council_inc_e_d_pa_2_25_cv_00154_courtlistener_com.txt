                                    COMPLAINT

I.     INTRODUCTION

       Plaintiff, Ferris Smith, brings this race discrimination, retaliation, and hostile

work environment action against her former employers, Law School Admission Council,

Inc. and Law School Admission Council (collectively, “Defendants” or “LSAC”).

Defendants failed to promote Plaintiff and terminated Plaintiff’s employment after she

complained about the race discriminatory conduct to which she and other Black

employees were subjected. Defendants’ discriminatory and retaliatory conduct towards

Plaintiff violated Title VII of the Civil Rights Act of 1964, as amended, 42 U.S.C. §

2000e, et seq. (“Title VII”); 42 U.S.C. §1981 (“Section 1981”); the Pennsylvania Human
      Case 2:25-cv-00154-KSM         Document 1      Filed 01/10/25       Page 2 of 16




Relations Act, as amended, 43 P.S. § 951, et seq. (“PHRA”); and the Philadelphia Fair

Practices Ordinance, as amended, Phila. Code § 9-1100, et seq. (“PFPO”). Plaintiff seeks

damages, including economic, compensatory, and punitive damages and all other

appropriate relief.

        28.    On or around July 7, 2022, Plaintiff complained of race discrimination to

Winfield in connection with how Plaintiff was being treated by Lopez.

        29.    Plaintiff stated to Winfield that she believed if she were a White woman,
