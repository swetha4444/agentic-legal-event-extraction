                       JURISDICTION AND VENUE

    1.    This action is brought pursuant to 28 U.S.C. §1332 and

the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000.00 and there is

complete diversity of citizenship between the parties hereto as

set forth herein below.

    2.    Venue lies in the United States District Court for the

Eastern District of Pennsylvania pursuant to 28 U.S.C. §1391

because all of the acts and/or omissions giving rise to the

claims set forth herein occurred in this Judicial District and

Defendant is located in this Judicial District.
